A taste of Taft Cuisine

Tinuhog ni Benny Barbecue House

Tinuhog ni Benny Barbecue House is a popular and affordable spot on Dagonoy St. known for its grilled Filipino dishes. Its pork sisig and grilled liempo are flavorful standouts, while the salmon belly sinigang is overly salty. Overall, it is a worthwhile choice for students craving filling Filipino food.

DAD'S MAMI

DAD’S MAMI is what one could consider a literal hole in the wall—a sweet spot to satisfy stomach grumbles without breaking the bank. Located just underneath the Vito Cruz LRT station, it serves as a point of respite for commuters who have had quite a trip or those with a long one ahead. Entering the place, guests are greeted by the white walls with an invitation to relax and unwind with the Filipino noodle house’s bowls of comfort food.
